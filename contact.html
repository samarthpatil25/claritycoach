Questions people ask

Is the discovery call really free?

Yes. It's a 30-minute conversation at no cost, and there's no obligation to book anything afterwards.

What do sessions cost after that?

You pay one session at a time, with no packages or subscription. The cost is shown when you book a slot, so you always see it before confirming, and nothing is charged before you decide to continue.

How long is a session?

The discovery call is 30 minutes. Full sessions are 60–90 minutes, online and one-on-one.

Do I need to prepare anything?

Just the short form you'll get after your first message. Come as you are otherwise.

A note before you message

These are coaching conversations, not a crisis or emergency service. If you're in distress or need urgent help, please contact a qualified mental-health professional or a local helpline right away.

These sessions offer coaching and conversational support. They are not psychotherapy, counselling, medical, or mental-health treatment, and are not a substitute for care from a licensed professional. If you are in crisis or need clinical help, please reach out to a qualified mental-health professional or a helpline.
