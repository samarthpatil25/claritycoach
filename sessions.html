What happens after the free call

The discovery call runs about 30 minutes. Nothing is decided during it. Afterwards you take your own time, and if you want to continue, you book the next call yourself.

You pay one session at a time.

No packages, no subscription, no commitment to a number of sessions. You book the next one only when you want it, and the cost is shown when you choose your slot, so nothing is a surprise.
